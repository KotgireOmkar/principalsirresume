About Me

I'm Dr. Vishweshwar

Principal & Pharmacologist

I am Dr. Dharashive Vishweshwar Mahishankar, a PhD in Pharmacology from Swami Ramanand Teerth Marathwada University, Nanded, and M.Pharm in Pharmacology from Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, Bangalore. Currently serving as Principal at Shivlingeshwar College of Pharmacy, Almala, Latur. My research focuses on Antiatherosclerotic and Cardioprotective studies of Indian Medicinal Plants. Specialized in Advanced Pharmacology, Toxicology, Molecular Biology, and Pharmacological Screening Methods.
